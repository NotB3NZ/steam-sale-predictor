# Research timeline — what happened, and why it mattered

This chronology reconstructs the completed work from local phase reports and receipts. Phase names were reused between the original pilot and the later cohort work; “pilot” and “cohort” distinguish them here. Dates appear only where explicitly documented. UTC receipt dates and local preparation dates are identified rather than treated as interchangeable. The chapter order is an explanatory structure, not a claim that Phase 8D EDA preceded model development.

## Boundaries on the chronology

Early root-level project documents contain statements such as “live expansion has not started.” They describe earlier checkpoints; the later batch receipts and cohort reports establish the completed expansion. They are preserved rather than rewritten to appear contemporaneous with the final report.

The canceled handpicked-game expansion is not part of the research sample. The synthesis follows the original frozen 2,000 AppIDs only; the two name discrepancies in the prediction CSV do not add or verify new titles.

There is no documented independently verified 2026 outcome evaluation to place at the end of this timeline. The story ends with qualified retrospective predictions, completed descriptive diagnostics and a synthesis ready for editorial work—not a prospective forecasting success claim.
